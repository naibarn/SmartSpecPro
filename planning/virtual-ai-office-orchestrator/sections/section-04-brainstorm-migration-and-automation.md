# Section 04: Brainstorm Migration And Automation

## Goal

Absorb brainstorm into the team discussion model and connect assistant teams to workflow, presentation, video, browser, and agency execution.

## Deliverables

- brainstorm-as-template migration path
- automation handoff contract
- approval and governance checkpoints

## Key Rule

Multi-agent discussion should produce inspectable artifacts and execution-ready outcomes, not only transcripts.
