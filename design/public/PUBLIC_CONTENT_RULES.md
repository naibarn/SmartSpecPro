# SmartAIHub public content rules

- Every capability statement maps to a current route or source-backed implementation entry in `implementation/public-claims-registry.json`.
- Route existence proves navigation only. It does not prove availability, output quality, customer adoption, pricing, legal rights, security posture, or service levels.
- Do not claim Film as a separate public product. Vertical Series is the supported flagship destination and uses the existing login return URL.
- Treat the public Gallery as live community/user content unless a moderation/campaign owner explicitly curates and approves a specific item.
- Keep Thai and English copy aligned in meaning, route destination, disclaimers, and availability caveats.
- Refresh route, claim, and media registries whenever page copy, links, media, or crawl policy changes.
