# Public UI anti-patterns

- Do not market the site as a Film-only or video-only generator.
- Do not invent a `/film` route or imply an unverified capability.
- Do not use live user gallery content as a customer testimonial or curated result.
- Do not ship assets as product/customer evidence without source, license/rights, tenant-safety and capture provenance.
- Do not use generic AI-SaaS gradients as a substitute for explaining the actual product flow.
- Do not use unexplained blank columns, tiny centered copy on wide displays, or unrelated repeated hero imagery.
- Do not let a CMS tenant page override SmartAIHub's platform homepage.
- Do not add global Astryx resets or global token overrides to solve a page-local problem.
- Do not hide essential explanation in animation, hover-only content, or media.
