# Public human imagery rules

Package 1.0.0 had no selected human likeness. The current repository-owned 1.2.0 candidate uses one user-authorized AI-generated editorial illustration beside the code-rendered product flow. It does not portray a real person, customer, employee, expert, or testimonial, and its provenance record is not an independent legal opinion.

Every human-asset publication requires a recorded source/generator, date, usage scope, consent or license basis, synthetic-person classification, art-direction approval, rights/likeness review, product-message review, accessibility/performance review, publication owner, withdrawal owner, and a tested withdrawal path. No generated person may be portrayed as an actual customer, employee, or testimonial subject.

Unknown provenance or rights means `UNVERIFIED`: exclude from production or retain only where the truth registry explicitly permits a disclosed non-evidentiary illustration. Never describe such an asset as a product capture, customer work, benchmark, or result.
