# Public human imagery rules

No human or customer likeness is selected for public Home package 1.0.0. Human-centered messaging is expressed through task intent and product flow, not implied testimonials or stock-photo faces.

If human imagery is proposed later, publication requires a recorded source/generator, date, usage scope, consent or license basis, synthetic-person classification, art-direction approval, rights/likeness approval, product-message approval, accessibility/performance review, CMS publication owner, withdrawal owner, and a tested withdrawal path. No generated person may be portrayed as an actual customer, employee, or testimonial subject.

Unknown provenance or rights means `UNVERIFIED`: exclude from production or retain only where the truth registry explicitly permits a disclosed non-evidentiary illustration. Never describe such an asset as a product capture, customer work, benchmark, or result.
