# Human imagery experiment guardrails

- No experiment may introduce a human likeness without a complete asset registry record and named approvals.
- Do not infer identity, demographics, emotion, or customer status from synthetic imagery.
- Do not make conversion, performance, or trust claims from an experiment without an approved analytics schema, privacy review, sample policy, and data-quality result.
- Respect consent state and honor withdrawal across source, derivatives, cached copies, and campaign variants.
- If any gate is missing, use the established non-human product-flow illustration and do not run the variant.
