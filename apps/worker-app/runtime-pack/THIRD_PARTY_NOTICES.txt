Smart AI Hub Worker Runtime Pack

Bundled components in the official runtime release:

- Official HyperFrames render sidecar for Smart AI Hub Worker App
- Google Chrome for Testing / Chromium runtime
- FFmpeg and ffprobe binaries
- Noto Sans Thai font

The bundled Noto Sans Thai font is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Its license text is included in the packaged runtime under `fonts/OFL.txt`.

The bundled browser and media binaries retain their upstream redistributable
license terms. Keep the packaged archive and manifest together when publishing
or mirroring the runtime release.
