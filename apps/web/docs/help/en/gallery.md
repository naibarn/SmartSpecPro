# Gallery

## What is the Gallery

The Gallery is a curated, publicly visible feed of AI-generated images, videos, and audio created by SmartAI Hub users. It is designed for discovery, inspiration, and showcasing your best work. Browsing is open to anyone; publishing requires a logged-in account.

## Browsing the Gallery

- The default view shows **recent public submissions** sorted by newest first.
- Use the **type filter** (Images, Videos, Audio) to narrow results.
- Use the **search bar** to find content by prompt keywords or tags.
- Switch between **Grid**, **Masonry**, and **List** layouts to suit your screen.
- Click any item to open the full detail view with the generation prompt, model, and creator.

## Featured content

- The **Featured** tab shows curated highlights selected by the platform team.
- Featured items appear at the top of the default feed and receive higher visibility.
- Any publicly submitted item may be featured — quality, originality, and prompt creativity are the main selection criteria.

## Engagement features

- **Like** — click the heart icon to save an item to your liked collection.
- **View count** — visible on each card; shows how many users have opened the detail view.
- **Download** — download the original-resolution file directly from the detail view.
- **Copy prompt** — copy the generation prompt to use as a starting point for your own variation.

## Publishing your work

To share a generated item to the Gallery:

1. Open the item in **Media Studio**, **Media History**, or **Document Management**.
2. Click **Share → Publish to Gallery**.
3. Add optional **tags** and a **title** before confirming.
4. The item becomes visible in the public Gallery within seconds.

You can also publish directly from a chat generation result using the share icon in the message.

## Privacy and removal

- Only items you explicitly publish appear in the Gallery — private files and chat attachments are never shown automatically.
- The generation **prompt** is shown publicly alongside the item. Avoid submitting items generated from sensitive or confidential prompts.
- **Unpublish** an item at any time from its Gallery detail view or from Document Management → the item is removed from the public feed immediately.
- Items deleted from your library are automatically unpublished from the Gallery.

## Tips

- Include descriptive tags when publishing to improve discoverability.
- Use **Copy Prompt** on gallery items you admire to study effective prompt techniques.
- The Gallery feed refreshes in real time — newly published items appear without a page reload.
