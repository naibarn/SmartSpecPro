# Agency Management

## Overview

The Agency Management page lets administrators review, approve, or reject agency submissions before they are published to the platform. Each agency consists of a team of AI agents working together.

## Reviewing agencies

The main table shows all submitted agencies with:

- **Name** and description
- **Agent count** — number of AI agents in the agency
- **Status** — pending, approved, rejected, or published
- **Fee/revenue sharing** details
- **Submission date**

Click a row to expand the full agency detail.

## Approval workflow

1. Review the agency's description, agent list, and configuration.
2. Check the revenue sharing terms and pricing.
3. Click **Approve** to accept the agency for publication.
4. Or click **Reject** with an optional note explaining why.

Approved agencies appear in the Agency Marketplace for users to browse and use.

## Managing published agencies

- **Unpublish** — temporarily remove an agency from the marketplace without deleting it.
- **Feature** — mark an agency as featured to highlight it on the marketplace homepage.
- **Edit** — modify agency settings, pricing, or description.

## Revenue and fees

Each agency can define:

- **Per-use fee** — credits charged each time a user runs the agency.
- **Revenue split** — percentage between the platform and the agency creator.

These are displayed during review and can be adjusted before approval.
