# Groups

## What are groups?

Groups are collaborative spaces where teams and communities can work together, share conversations, and pool resources. A group can represent a work team, a project community, a topic-based club, or any other collection of people who want to collaborate in a shared space.

## Creating a group

1. Go to **Groups** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Group**.
3. Enter a name and a short description.
4. Choose visibility:
   - **Public** — anyone can discover and join the group
   - **Private** — only invited members can join; the group does not appear in discovery
5. Click **Create**.

You become the owner of the group automatically.

## Joining groups

- Go to **Groups → Discover** to browse public groups.
- Use the search bar to find groups by name or topic.
- Click **Join** on any public group to become a member immediately.
- For private groups, click **Request to Join** — the group owner or an admin must approve your request.
- You can also join a group via an invite link sent by a member.

## Group features

Once inside a group, members share access to:

- **Conversations** — dedicated chat threads visible to all group members
- **Resources** — shared files, links, and reference documents uploaded by members
- **Members** — a directory of all group participants and their roles
- **Activity feed** — a summary of recent group activity

## Managing members

Group owners and admins can:

- **Invite members** — send an invite link or search by username/email
- **Remove members** — remove anyone who is not an owner
- **Set roles** — assign the **Admin** role to trusted members so they can help manage the group
- **Approve join requests** — for private groups, review and accept or decline incoming requests

To manage members, go to the group page and click **Members → Manage**.

## Group discovery

The **Discover** page at **Groups → Discover** shows public groups sorted by activity and member count. You can:

- Filter by topic tags
- Search by keyword
- Sort by newest, most active, or most members

## Tips

- **Use a descriptive name** — a clear name helps people understand the group's purpose before joining.
- **Set a clear description** — explain what the group is for and who it is for in the description field.
- **Start with private** — if your group is for an internal team, set it to private so membership stays controlled.
- **Assign admins early** — give at least one other person admin rights so the group can keep running if you are unavailable.
- **Use shared resources** — upload reference documents or links to the group's Resources tab so all members have easy access.
