# Getting Started with SmartAI Hub

SmartAI Hub is an AI-powered platform for content creation, research, media generation, and intelligent automation. This guide walks you through the core surfaces and helps you get productive quickly.

## What you can do

- **Chat with AI** — Ask questions, draft content, brainstorm ideas, and analyze material using the world's best language models.
- **Generate media** — Create images, videos, and audio from text descriptions.
- **Build presentations** — Turn a chat request into a full slide deck in seconds.
- **Run agencies** — Deploy multi-agent teams to tackle complex research, storyboarding, and comparison tasks.
- **Browse the web with AI** — Use Browser Session to have an AI navigate websites, gather data, and surface structured results for you.

## How to navigate

| Area | How to reach it | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Chat | Click **Chat** in the sidebar | Conversations, skills, media generation |
| Media Studio | Click **Media** in the sidebar | Review and manage generated media |
| Presentations | Click **Presentations** in the sidebar | Edit and export slide decks |
| Agency | Click **Agencies** in the toolbar inside Chat | Launch multi-agent workflows |
| Browser Session | Click **Browser Session** inside Chat | AI-powered web browsing |
| Settings | Click your avatar → Settings | Preferences, memory, personas |
| Credits | Click **Credits** in the sidebar | Check balance, top up |

## Recommended first steps

1. Open **Chat** and type a question or a task description.
2. Try typing `/` to open the skill menu and explore available workflows.
3. Ask for an image by typing `create image: a futuristic city at sunset`.
4. Click **Agencies** to explore multi-agent templates like Deep Research or Deck Builder.
5. Visit **Settings → Memory** to see how the AI remembers your preferences.

## Dashboard

After logging in, the Dashboard shows:

- **Credit balance** — your remaining credits at a glance.
- **Recent conversations** — quickly resume where you left off.
- **Quick actions** — shortcuts to Chat, Media Studio, and Presentations.
- **Usage overview** — your activity summary.

## Getting help

- Click the **?** (Help) button on any page to open contextual help.
- Visit the Help Center at `/help` for all topics.
- Ask questions in Chat — type "how do I..." and the help assistant will answer from the documentation.

## Quick tips

- Start in **Chat** for most tasks — it is the fastest entry point.
- Move to **Browser Session** when you need live website data.
- Move to **Agencies** when your task needs a structured deliverable (report, deck, storyboard).
- Use **credits** to track and manage your AI usage. Each model request consumes credits based on token usage.
- Switch language (EN/TH) from Settings or the Help panel language toggle.
