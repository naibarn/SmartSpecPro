## Product Fidelity Lock

Lock exact appliance type, body volume, door/lid/basket/blade/vent/panel placement, handle shape, controls, cord/plug visibility, material, and scale class.

## Common Wrong Substitutions To Reject

Do not change appliance class, capacity impression, door direction, control layout, vents, basket/lid geometry, or convert the item into a premium-looking different model.

## Storyboard Use Context

Show kitchen, laundry, cleaning, air, or home-use proof scenes that match the storyboard and avoid unsafe operation or unsupported performance claims.
