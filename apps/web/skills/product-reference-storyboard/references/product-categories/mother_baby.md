# Mother and baby (สินค้าแม่และเด็ก)

Category id: `mother_baby`
Legacy skill: `mother-baby-reference-storyboard`

## Product Fidelity Lock

Lock exact baby product class, safety-visible parts, straps/rails/wheels/bottle nipple/lid count, the material exactly as shown in the product reference image, colorway, size class, and age/use context. The product reference image is the source of truth for material and finish: hard moulded plastic stays hard moulded plastic, metal stays metal, wood stays wood, and only genuinely padded or textile parts are soft. Never assume soft padding, quilting, plush, or fabric upholstery just because the product is for babies or children.

## Common Wrong Substitutions To Reject

Do not add unsupported safety claims, unsafe use, wrong age impression, extra restraints, missing straps, different stroller/chair/bottle structure, or unrealistic baby handling. Do not convert hard moulded plastic seats, trays, rails, backs, or shells into padded fabric cushions, quilted upholstery, stitched piping, or plush textile surfaces, and do not add fabric covers, padding, or cloth texture that is not visible in the product reference image. A printed graphic on a hard plastic surface must stay a printed graphic on hard plastic — never re-render it as embroidery, appliqué, or a sewn cushion.

## Storyboard Use Context

Use calm nursery, feeding, stroller, care, storage, or parent-hand scenes; if a child appears, keep it safe, supervised, realistic, and secondary to product truth.

## Frame-Level Requirements

- Put the category-specific lock facts in the shared PRODUCT VERIFY block; use only a short product-use cue inside product-visible frames.
- Wide, overview, result, confirmation, and CTA frames must show enough of the full silhouette to verify product class, countable parts, material, scale, and colorway.
- Detail close-ups may crop tighter, but they must still show adjacent geometry or context that proves the product did not change. A close-up must also preserve the exact surface material and finish from the product reference image (plastic gloss or matte, moulding seams, printed graphics, edge profile). Close-ups are the frames most likely to invent a softer, plusher, more premium material than the real product, so state the true material explicitly in every close-up frame prompt.
- Environment references may influence setting and light only; they must not provide a replacement product from this category.
- If a person appears with the product, use clear front-facing or three-quarter face continuity unless the beat is deliberately hands-only/POV/top-down without visible identity.
