# Mother and baby (สินค้าแม่และเด็ก)

Category id: `mother_baby`
Legacy skill: `mother-baby-reference-storyboard`

## Product Fidelity Lock

Lock exact baby product class, safety-visible parts, straps/rails/wheels/bottle nipple/lid count, soft material, colorway, size class, and age/use context.

## Common Wrong Substitutions To Reject

Do not add unsupported safety claims, unsafe use, wrong age impression, extra restraints, missing straps, different stroller/chair/bottle structure, or unrealistic baby handling.

## Storyboard Use Context

Use calm nursery, feeding, stroller, care, storage, or parent-hand scenes; if a child appears, keep it safe, supervised, realistic, and secondary to product truth.

## Frame-Level Requirements

- Put the category-specific lock facts in the shared PRODUCT VERIFY block; use only a short product-use cue inside product-visible frames.
- Wide, overview, result, confirmation, and CTA frames must show enough of the full silhouette to verify product class, countable parts, material, scale, and colorway.
- Detail close-ups may crop tighter, but they must still show adjacent geometry or context that proves the product did not change.
- Environment references may influence setting and light only; they must not provide a replacement product from this category.
- If a person appears with the product, use clear front-facing or three-quarter face continuity unless the beat is deliberately hands-only/POV/top-down without visible identity.
