# Furniture (เฟอร์นิเจอร์)

Category id: `furniture`
Legacy skill: `furniture-reference-storyboard`

## Product Fidelity Lock

Lock exact furniture subtype, dimensions/scale class, tier/shelf/drawer/door/cushion/leg/post count, joinery, hardware, material, finish, colorway, and room footprint.

## Common Wrong Substitutions To Reject

Do not convert open shelves into cabinets, add drawers/doors, change tier count, resize compact furniture, swap material, copy similar background furniture, or simplify construction.

For bedside tables, nightstands, open shelves, racks, and compact storage furniture, reject any drawer nightstand, closed cabinet, alternate lamp table, solid-sided cabinet, generic bedside table, or environment-provided replacement when the locked product is an open multi-tier shelf. If Product Detail says a 3-tier bedside shelf, Frame 8 / reconfirming-value / confirmation frames must show the same 3-tier open shelf with top surface, middle shelf, bottom shelf, open sides/posts, light wood finish, compact bedside scale, and no drawers or doors.

## Storyboard Use Context

Use realistic room-scale scenes with enough wide/three-quarter views to verify silhouette and countable parts; close-ups must still imply the same structure.

## Frame-Level Requirements

- Repeat the category-specific lock inside every product-visible frame together with the exact Product Detail facts.
- Wide, overview, result, confirmation, and CTA frames must show enough of the full silhouette to verify product class, countable parts, material, scale, and colorway.
- Detail close-ups may crop tighter, but they must still show adjacent geometry or context that proves the product did not change.
- Environment references may influence setting and light only; they must not provide a replacement product from this category.
- If a person appears with the product, use clear front-facing or three-quarter face continuity unless the beat is deliberately hands-only/POV/top-down without visible identity.
