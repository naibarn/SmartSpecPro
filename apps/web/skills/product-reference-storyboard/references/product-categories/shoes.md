# Shoes (รองเท้า)

Category id: `shoes`
Legacy skill: `shoes-reference-storyboard`

## Product Fidelity Lock

Lock exact footwear type, pair count, sole thickness/tread, toe shape, heel height, lace/strap/buckle layout, upper material, color blocking, and logo/marking placement.

## Common Wrong Substitutions To Reject

Do not convert sneakers to running shoes, sandals to slippers, change sole geometry, add/remove laces or straps, or show only one shoe unless the product is one-piece.

## Storyboard Use Context

Use realistic on-foot, walking, detail, storage, try-on, or outfit scenes while keeping both product identity and human proportions believable.

## Frame-Level Requirements

- Repeat the category-specific lock inside every product-visible frame together with the exact Product Detail facts.
- Wide, overview, result, confirmation, and CTA frames must show enough of the full silhouette to verify product class, countable parts, material, scale, and colorway.
- Detail close-ups may crop tighter, but they must still show adjacent geometry or context that proves the product did not change.
- Environment references may influence setting and light only; they must not provide a replacement product from this category.
- If a person appears with the product, use clear front-facing or three-quarter face continuity unless the beat is deliberately hands-only/POV/top-down without visible identity.
