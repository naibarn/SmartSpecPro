# Cinematic Knowledge Base for Video Prompt Engineer

## 🎬 Camera Movements (Professional Terms)

### Sora & Kling (Technical Terms Supported)

**Dolly Movements:**
- `Dolly In` - Camera physically moves closer to subject
- `Dolly Out` - Camera physically moves away from subject
- `Dolly Zoom` - Dolly + zoom simultaneously (Vertigo effect)

**Pan & Tilt:**
- `Pan Left/Right` - Camera rotates horizontally on fixed axis
- `Tilt Up/Down` - Camera rotates vertically on fixed axis
- `Whip Pan` - Extremely fast pan creating motion blur

**Tracking & Following:**
- `Tracking Shot` - Camera follows subject's movement
- `Trucking Shot` - Camera moves parallel to subject
- `Arc Shot` - Camera circles around subject

**Vertical Movements:**
- `Crane Shot` - Camera rises or descends vertically
- `Boom Shot` - Similar to crane, often faster
- `Pedestal Up/Down` - Camera height adjustment

**Special Movements:**
- `Handheld` - Organic, slightly shaky movement
- `Steadicam` - Smooth, flowing handheld
- `Gimbal Shot` - Ultra-smooth stabilized movement
- `Drone Shot` - Aerial perspective with smooth movement

### Veo & Wan (Simple Terms)

- `Camera moves closer` - Approaching subject
- `Camera pulls back` - Revealing more context
- `Camera pans across` - Horizontal sweep
- `Camera tilts up/down` - Vertical movement
- `Camera follows` - Tracking subject
- `Camera circles around` - Orbiting movement

## 📐 Shot Compositions

### Close-Ups
- **Extreme Close-Up (ECU)** - Eyes, lips, or detail
- **Close-Up (CU)** - Full face or single object
- **Medium Close-Up (MCU)** - Head and shoulders

### Medium Shots
- **Medium Shot (MS)** - Waist up
- **Medium Full Shot** - Knees up
- **Cowboy Shot** - Mid-thigh up

### Wide Shots
- **Wide Shot (WS)** - Full body in environment
- **Extreme Wide Shot (EWS)** - Vast landscape
- **Establishing Shot` - Setting the scene

### Special Angles
- **Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)** - View from behind person
- **Point of View (POV)** - Character's perspective
- **Dutch Angle` - Tilted frame (tension, unease)
- **Low Angle` - Looking up (power, dominance)
- **High Angle` - Looking down (vulnerability, weakness)
- **Bird's Eye View` - Directly overhead
- **Worm's Eye View` - Ground level looking up

## 💡 Lighting Styles

### Natural Lighting
- **Golden Hour** - Warm, soft light (sunrise/sunset)
- **Blue Hour** - Cool, ethereal light (twilight)
- **Overcast** - Soft, even lighting
- **Direct Sunlight** - Strong, harsh shadows

### Studio Lighting
- **Three-Point Lighting** - Key + fill + back
- **Rembrandt Lighting** - Triangle of light on cheek
- **Butterfly Lighting** - Shadow under nose
- **Loop Lighting** - Small shadow beside nose

### Dramatic Lighting
- **Low-Key** - High contrast, dark shadows
- **High-Key** - Bright, minimal shadows
- **Chiaroscuro` - Strong light/dark contrast
- **Silhouette` - Backlit subject, dark foreground

### Special Effects
- **Neon** - Artificial colored lights
- **Practical Lights** - Visible light sources in frame
- **Volumetric Lighting** - Light beams through atmosphere
- **Rim Lighting` - Light outlining subject's edge
- **Backlighting` - Light behind subject

## 🎨 Visual Styles & Color Grading

### Film Styles
- **Cinematic** - Film-like, 24fps feel, slight grain
- **Documentary` - Observational, handheld, natural
- **Commercial` - Polished, vibrant, perfect
- **Music Video` - Stylized, artistic, dynamic

### Color Grades
- **Teal & Orange** - Hollywood blockbuster
- **Bleach Bypass** - Desaturated, high contrast
- **Vintage Film** - Grain, faded colors, vignette
- **Noir** - Black and white, high contrast
- **Cyberpunk` - Neon colors, dark backgrounds
- **Pastel` - Soft, muted, dreamy colors

### Lens Characteristics
- **Anamorphic** - Widescreen, oval bokeh, lens flares
- **Vintage Lens** - Soft focus, warm tones, character
- **Tilt-Shift** - Miniature effect, selective focus

## 📱 Platform-Specific Best Practices

### TikTok (9:16)
**Hook:** First 0-3 seconds critical
**Pacing:** Fast cuts, dynamic
**Audio:** Trending sounds important
**Length:** 15-60 seconds optimal
**Text:** Large, readable on mobile
**Trends:** Jump cuts, transitions

### Instagram Reels (9:16, 4:5)
**Aesthetic:** Clean, beautiful
**First Frame:** Must be strong
**Transitions:** Smooth, professional
**Audio:** Original music preferred
**Length:** 15-30 seconds optimal
**Branding:** Subtle but present

### YouTube Shorts (9:16)
**Hook:** Question or surprise
**Value:** Clear takeaway
**Length:** 15-60 seconds
**CTA:** Strong end screen
**Audio:** Clear voiceover

### YouTube Standard (16:9)
**Quality:** Cinematic, high production
**Story:** Longer narrative arc
**Audio:** Professional mixing
**Length:** 2-10+ minutes
**Chapters:** Clear structure

## 🎬 Pacing & Rhythm

### Fast Pacing
- Quick cuts (1-2 seconds)
- High energy
- Multiple angles
- Upbeat music
- For: Action, product showcases

### Medium Pacing
- Balanced cuts (2-4 seconds)
- Natural rhythm
- Varied shots
- Versatile music
- For: Most content types

### Slow Pacing
- Long takes (4-8+ seconds)
- Contemplative
- Wide shots
- Ambient music
- For: Emotional, artistic

### Variable Pacing
- Mix of fast and slow
- Builds tension
- Release points
- Dynamic music
- For: Storytelling, drama

- Seamless transition
- Professional feel

- Smooth narrative flow
- Professional editing

- Multiple actions simultaneously
- Comparison
- Before/after

- Speed ramps
