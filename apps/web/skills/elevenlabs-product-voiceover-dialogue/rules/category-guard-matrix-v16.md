# Category Guard Matrix v16

This matrix extends v15 and runs before final output.

## New/strengthened categories

1. Medical-device scar gel / film-forming silicone gel
- Say: film-forming silicone gel, clean/appropriate skin, label directions, thin layer, expiry or permit only as neutral label facts.
- Do not say: prevents scars, removes scars, heals wounds, prevents infection, blocks bacteria, medical-grade superiority.
- Add: consult a medical professional for deep, infected, painful, abnormal, or open wounds.

2. HPV self-sampling / diagnostic kit
- Say: self-sampling service kit, follow instructions, collect/ship as directed, results sent through stated channel when provided.
- Do not say: diagnosis guaranteed, replaces screening by clinician, detects/cures HPV, medical advice.
- Add: read device instructions and discuss results or symptoms with a healthcare professional.

3. Hair-loss/scalp-care set with supplement
- Say: hair/scalp care routine, volume look, refreshing scalp feel, supplement as separate item only if necessary.
- Do not say: reduces hair loss, regrows hair, stimulates follicles, better than Minoxidil, extends anagen, increases hair count/weight.
- Add for supplement: eat a variety of foods from all five food groups, read label, not disease prevention/treatment, consult professional when pregnant/ill/on medication.

4. Acne/dandruff/scalp-condition shampoo or serum
- Say: cleansing scalp, fresh feel, oil-prone/flaky-feeling scalp routine, moisturizing scalp feel.
- Do not say: treats acne, dandruff, seborrheic dermatitis, fungus, bacteria, inflammation, itch cure, clinical reduction.
- Add: consult a professional if redness, itch, flaking, wounds, or hair fall persists.

5. Intimate cleanser
- Say: external cleansing, pH/free-from label facts, freshness, gentle routine.
- Do not say: treats discharge, infection, bacterial imbalance, odor disease, itching, irritation, or gynecological symptoms.
- Add: external use only; see professional for unusual odor, discharge, itch, or discomfort.

6. UV apparel / textile UPF
- Say: UPF label fact, coverage, light layer for outdoor routine.
- Do not say: absolute UV block, prevents skin darkening/damage, medical sun protection guarantee.
- Add: use sunscreen and sun-safe habits for exposed skin.

7. Whitening lotion/mist repeated guard
- Say: moisturized, soft, glowy, fresh-looking, radiant-looking.
- Do not say: skin turns white, whitening in days, fades spots, changes natural skin tone.

8. Clinical-number/evidence simplifier
- Percentages, x-times, trial counts, research/doctor references, and hospital references are not spoken as ad hooks.
- Convert to natural benefit copy and keep only neutral label facts when necessary.
