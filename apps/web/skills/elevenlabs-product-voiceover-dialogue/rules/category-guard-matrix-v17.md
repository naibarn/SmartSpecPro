# Category Guard Matrix v17

## New / strengthened categories

- `set_bundle_multi_product`: Split products by role. Do not merge claims. Include routine-based journey only when logical.
- `dietary_supplement_effervescent`: Use food-complement framing. Include 5-food-group reminder. Avoid disease prevention/treatment.
- `underarm_brightening_set`: Use confidence/freshness/skin-care appearance language. Avoid white/whitening guarantees.
- `medical_device_scar_gel`: Focus on film-forming silicone use on appropriate clean skin. Recommend professional advice for open/deep/infected wounds.
- `hpv_sti_self_sampling`: Focus on reading instructions, correct collection, shipping window, confidentiality, and professional follow-up. No diagnosis.
- `uv_textile_apparel`: Use UPF/coverage language only as physical feature; results depend on fit, coverage, washing, and exposure.
- `hair_loss_refill_or_set`: Present as scalp cleansing, volume look, or routine support. Avoid hair growth, Minoxidil comparisons, or guaranteed reduced shedding.
- `oral_whitening_toothgel`: Frame as surface-stain care and fresh breath. Avoid instant/permanent whitening.
- `face_acne_wash`: Frame as cleansing routine with BHA/salicylic acid; avoid acne treatment guarantees.
- `body_lotion_perfumed_brightening`: Focus on scent, moisture, glow, soft skin; avoid whitening, spot removal, permanent tone change.

## Hard output rule
All final dialogue lines must sound like consumer-facing ad copy. Internal compliance notes must appear only in test reports, never in generated dialogue.
