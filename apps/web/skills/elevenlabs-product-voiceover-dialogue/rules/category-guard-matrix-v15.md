# Category Guard Matrix v15

This v15 matrix extends v14 and is used by the repair loop before final output.

## New/strengthened categories

1. Acne cushion / acne-prone makeup
- Say: coverage, oil-control look, shade choice, longwear as label context.
- Do not say: reduces acne, dermatologist research, 50% reduction, non-comedogenic guarantee.
- Add: remove thoroughly, patch test, professional advice if irritation/acne persists.

2. Concealer / color corrector
- Say: peach/blue/corrector use, coverage, under-eye looks brighter.
- Do not say: erases dark circles, treats pigmentation, no crease all day.
- Add: shade and finish vary by skin tone and application.

3. Urea cream / rough dry areas
- Say: urea percentage, moisturizing, smoother-feeling rough areas.
- Do not say: cures chicken skin, repairs barrier, guaranteed TEWL improvements.
- Add: avoid wounds, patch test, professional advice for pregnancy or skin conditions.

4. Mouth spray / propolis oral spray
- Say: fresh breath, portable oral-care routine, free-from list if stated.
- Do not say: treats sore throat, oral ulcers, infection, inflammation, bacteria kill.
- Add: consult dentist/doctor for persistent pain, sores, odor, or throat symptoms.

5. Retinal / retinoid body oil or cream
- Say: night care, moisturized/smoother-looking skin.
- Do not say: anti-aging reversal, wrinkle reduction, youthful-lock guarantee.
- Add: follow label, avoid use in pregnancy when label warns, sunscreen by day.

6. AHA/BHA body cleanser / face wash / body solution
- Say: cleansing, exfoliating routine, percentage as label fact.
- Do not say: acne reduction percentages, pore cure, whitening.
- Add: start slowly for leave-on acids, avoid eyes/wounds, use sunscreen.

7. UV hand cream / sunscreen hand cream
- Say: SPF/PA facts, soft hands, daily care, reapply after washing.
- Do not say: anti-aging result, spots/freckles fade, hypoallergenic guarantee.

8. Anti-dust-mite pillowcase
- Say: microfiber, zip, helps reduce contact with dust/allergens.
- Do not say: allergy treatment, 99.99% protection, anti-bacterial/fungal guarantee.
- Add: wash as directed, not medical treatment.

## Global v15 rule
Clinical, dermatologist, hospital, percentage, and x-times claims are not used as spoken sales hooks unless the user explicitly requests an evidence-focused factual script. Default ad scripts should be natural and customer-facing.
