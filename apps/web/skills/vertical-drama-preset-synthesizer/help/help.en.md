# Vertical Drama Preset Synthesizer

This skill blends several selected story flavors, such as restaurant service, customer/staff comedy, local business drama, and everyday lifestyle, into one editable Vertical Drama preset draft.

It does not auto-trigger, does not generate media, and returns structured JSON that the app validates before showing it in the Create Series Wizard.

Before applying a draft, the wizard shows a plain-language summary: what the story is about, who leads it and why, what conflict or discovery they face, the central mystery, and decision notes. Technical blend mechanics stay collapsed by default. Each character must have a story role (lead, villain, supporting, or equivalent) separate from their occupation.
