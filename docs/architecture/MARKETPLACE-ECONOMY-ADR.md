# ADR — Mini App Marketplace, Four-Role Credit Income and Credit V2 (R0.8), 2026-11-01

Owner decisions: First-party/third-party Mini App and freelancer-like Service Marketplace prioritize creator portfolios and useful products over a purely agent marketplace. Two price offers: Skill pay-per-use (meter every Skill and apply configured payer price), Mini App subscription (only newly authored exclusive Skills owned/bound to this App are included at zero extra subscriber credits; ALL shared Skills charge credits at each distinct invocation). Canonical revenue roles are exactly Platform, Tenant Infrastructure, Mini App Owner and Skill Owner; referral is NEVER a revenue recipient. No fixed split percent without signed agreement and cost model.

Economy unit change: OLD $1=1000 credits, NEW $1=10000 credits. Default economic preservation: 1 old credit equals 10 new. Existing historical ledger entries remain historical; ledger+balance migration must be idempotent and preserve funds. Scale all old nominal price/grant/reserve/refund/threshold factors by 10 absent an explicitly separate change of value. If old UI/worker writes same balance concurrently at old units, use version bridge or coordinated cutover. No frontend-only scaling trick.

Four revenue roles represent economic attribution, not mandatory unique persons. Every recognized role share is credited into the verified recipient USER wallet via the existing canonical credit ledger, preserving distinct attribution even when roles map to one account. Taxes and processor fees are expense/tax categories rather than recipients. Affiliate/Referral is a one-time Platform-funded promotional wallet credit after a verified qualifying event, NEVER a recurring revenue percentage or fifth party. Agency/co-creator contracts are fulfilled by appropriate rights binding or independently negotiated services, not by introducing extra per-call split slots. White-label BYOC Cloudflare account does not automatically decide merchant-of-record or wallet ownership. Avoid claiming escrow if payment-provider authorization/regulations unverified.

Subscription revenue allocation: versioned Platform/Tenant/App shares plus any separately contracted royalty for newly authored **App-exclusive** Skill creators. **No subscription-funded general/shared Skill royalty pool**: shared/Core/Marketplace/other-App Skills have a separate per-invocation price, debit and Skill-owner allocation, including nested calls. A retry is not a new billable invocation; an actual additional shared Skill call is. Protect creator economics from unbounded "unlimited AI" burn; publish fair-use/limits transparently. Use SPEC-207/280/303 existing authorities, not new ledgers.

Fastwork product inspiration: search/discover, creator profiles, portfolio, package/service listing, customer contact, trusted payment and reviews. Source: https://fastwork.com/about . Payment conceptual support https://stripe.com/resources/more/marketplace-payment-processing-apis .

Scope/provenance rule: the App-only Skill must have a distinct implementation written for one canonical App ID, not merely renamed/cloned/wrapped reusable Skill code. If it becomes available to another App or external workflow it is reclassified as shared, with clear quote and consent before further per-call charges. One Mini App may contain both included App-private and paid shared Skills; the listing and checkout must display them separately.

## R0.8 simplified recipient / withdrawal decision

Pay-per-use: payer credits debit once; four role shares become canonical **credit additions** to verified user wallets, conservation and idempotency enforced. Monthly Mini App subscription: one verified payment/period recognition funds one credit-equivalent distribution to roles; App-exclusive Skills remain included, shared Skills continue per-call billing. Referral: once per unique eligible referred user, credit award paid separately from the approved Platform promotion budget, no fifth share. A credit top-up is funding/liability, not four-party distributable income. One wallet UI can show credits from purchases, earnings and bonuses while transaction provenance keeps them distinguishable. **Cash withdrawal is postponed to a distinct future spec** with configurable minimums, identity, tax, FX and payout eligibility; no claim of withdrawable cash today.
