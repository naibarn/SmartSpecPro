# R0.8 — Credit-first Four-Role Earnings / Referral Correction

**Decision applied:** Affiliate/Referral is no longer an optional revenue participant; it is a distinct one-time credit award to the verified referrer user. All income through Mini App/Skill revenue allocation is credited to verified user wallets; no external cash payout now. Withdrawal design is deferred with future configurable minimum.

## Prior gaps removed

1. R0.7 SPEC-321 included optional affiliate/service recipients in the four-way allocation. Replaced with exactly four canonical roles; independent service agreements do not add fifth slot.
2. R0.7 waterfall ended in payout accrual/external settlement. Replaced with canonical user-wallet credit additions and no enabled withdrawal path.
3. No separation of referral from revenue. Added Platform-budgeted one-time promotional award with global referred-user uniqueness, idempotency, account-abuse checks and reversals.
4. Credit top-up could be conflated with earned revenue. Explicitly prohibit creator allocation on top-up; distribute only upon recognized paid usage or subscribed period.
5. Existing one-wallet design lacked future cash eligibility provenance. Preserve purchase/earning/referral/correction classes, pending flags and exact credit unit; do not implement cashouts until separate spec including minimum balance and compliance.
6. Subscription share could be paid for every invocation. Clarify single funded subscription-period allocation; App-exclusive Skills included, shared Skills per-call credit debit and recipient grants.
7. Multi-role same user could double-post. Preserve role receipts but consolidate user wallet delta correctly; retries/rollbacks use canonical idempotency.
8. Public Site and Codex instructions previously referred to payout; changed to future-only and reward credit guidance.

## Non-goals / important distinction

- This is a **policy/document revision**, not an executed data migration or production wallet credit grant.
- Revenue is an economic/legal event, while a posted SmartSIHub credit is an internal stored-value unit. The ledger must retain USD/THB source financial receipts and the outstanding liability; credits cannot automatically be treated as legally withdrawable cash.
- The owner has not selected split percentages, referral bonus amount, qualifying event exceptions, withdrawal minimums, cash-out FX, tax or processor agreements. The spec leaves these policy-configurable or future-design rather than fabricating rates.
- Affiliate referral should not be confused with marketer revenue from separately sold contracted services.

## Minimum UAT

- Four owner roles total; recipient wallet reflects exact credits after accepted charge, not an external payment.
- Referral earns exactly one separate wallet bonus from Platform budget, no share reduction.
- Subscribe -> period allocation once; exclusive Skill free per call; shared Skill debit and allocation on every distinct execution.
- Queue retries/payment webhook retries do not duplicate any debit, grant or bonus.
- Historical credit V1->V2 conversion remains consistent; withdrawal is unavailable.
