# Request

Continue from Feature 066 (Beam Billing & Invoice Phase 1) with the next delivery phase for SmartSpecPro billing.

Phase 2 should build on the Phase 1 billing foundation and define the next production step:

- saved card / card tokenization
- automatic monthly renewal charging
- dunning and retry policy
- card-focused recovery and customer self-service
- compliance and rollout constraints for stored payment methods

The deliverable should be implementation-ready enough to guide a follow-up `/deep-implement` round without redefining the billing foundation built in Phase 1.
