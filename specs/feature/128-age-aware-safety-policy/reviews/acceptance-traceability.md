## Open-Question Handling

Open questions in `spec.md` section 21 do not block implementation of adult-only/observe foundation. They are handled as follows:

- Questions 1, 9, 10, 11, 12, and 13 are legal/product/admin policy launch gates before age-tiered minor access.
- Questions 2 and 3 are implementation defaults: first setup may use the authenticated session; later DOB/country edits require protected PIN and rate-limited/audited change policy.
- Question 4 is implementation default: protected unlocks are scoped per surface/action by default.
- Question 5 is implementation default: use a compatibility adapter over existing Private Vault storage first; migrate storage later only with an explicit tested migration.
- Question 6 is implementation default: unknown users are sent to completion/interstitial flows where UX allows, but backend still enforces child-under-13.
- Questions 7 and 8 are represented by central policy and adult-only service mode defaults.

## Conclusion

All acceptance criteria have implementation-plan coverage. Remaining decisions are explicit launch gates, not blockers for implementing the adult-only/observe foundation and system-wide policy architecture.
