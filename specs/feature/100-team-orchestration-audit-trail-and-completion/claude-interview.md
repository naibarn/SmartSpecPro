# Stakeholder Interview Notes

No interactive interview was run in this session.

## Assumptions carried into the plan

- The primary user problem is auditability, not just visual polish.
- The Team page must present a work ledger first and a conversation feed second.
- `auto_team` should continue through review and rework loops until completion evidence exists, unless a policy stop condition is reached.
- Manual Team mode and auto Team mode should share the same underlying ledger model but use different default controls and emphasis.
- The user wants a full redesign, not a small incremental patch.
