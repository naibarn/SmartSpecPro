# Section 04 — Slot Presets, Custom Slots, and B-roll

Profile presets create suggested slots for place, restaurant, product,
software, documentary, and hybrid coverage. Each slot has a title and a short
creator-facing narrative description. Users can edit, reorder, remove, and add
custom slots.

The hub supports image and video assets. Video references carry duration,
trim-in/out, orientation, audio, rights/disclosure, and approval metadata.

Only approved assets with a production-allowed rights/disclosure status can be
bound to an episode/shot as establishing footage,
cutaway, insert, evidence, demonstration, transition, or B-roll overlay. The
story draft proposes a slot ID and usage; production resolves the managed asset
and validates the allowed usage and trim. It also validates asset existence,
ownership, source version, aspect/orientation, safe zones, overlay order, and
audio policy. External source audio is muted by default unless explicitly
approved with rights/disclosure metadata.

The binding is versioned and remains advisory until creator approval. A missing,
stale, unauthorized, or out-of-bounds binding fails closed with a repair item;
it is never replaced by a provider-only URL or invented media.

No asset is automatically injected into every episode. Asset usage cannot
rewrite story canon or relationship memory.
