## Closure decision

This first 12-round loop is complete. Follow-up spec-to-code review and fixes are recorded separately in `spec-compliance-12-rounds.md`. The Spec 278 implementation itself is **not complete**: sections 02–06, 08–11 have named partial/deferred gates, especially a process-persistent Session Host, recovery inventory/caller/issuance, platform containment, provider adapters, commercial authority, browser proof and certification. Do not enable the projection flag or claim a durable-session beta until those gates are implemented and verified.
