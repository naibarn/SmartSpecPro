## Goal

Add only the foundational new primitives that remain justified after presets and grouping exist.

## Recommended Follow-Up

- Prefer a dedicated `svg` or generalized `shape` element before considering many bespoke semantic widgets.

## Defer Until Proven Necessary

- table
- chart
- data timeline
- resume-specific or infographic-specific one-off element types

## Reason

These widgets have heavier editing and rendering requirements and should come after the composition model is strong enough to cover most visual use cases.
